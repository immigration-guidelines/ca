Canada - labour authority: Employment and Social Development Canada (ESDC) / Service Canada,
Temporary Foreign Worker Program (TFWP)
Collected 15 November 2026. Script: download_ca_labour.py (python3 download_ca_labour.py).

Role
ESDC/Service Canada officers assess Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA) applications; a positive LMIA is
the precondition for most employer-specific work permits, which IRCC then decides. In Quebec the LMIA is
assessed jointly with the province (MIFI).

What exists
ESDC does not publish its TFWP program directives, its LMIA assessment manual or officer training material.
They are known only from access-to-information releases (not published by ESDC as documents). What ESDC
publishes, and what its officers apply, are the "program requirements" pages of each TFWP stream and a few
cross-cutting policy pages on canada.ca (business legitimacy, refusal to process, wage and advertising
variations, employer compliance, modification and expiry of LMIAs, temporary measures). They are written for
employers, but they are the only public statement of the criteria ESDC applies. They are kept here as
guidance with that caveat; purely procedural pages (how to apply, after you apply), forms, worker leaflets
and statistics are left out. IRCC's own instructions on LMIA-based work permits are in ca/ (IRCC program
delivery instructions), not here.

Source and method
www.canada.ca did not answer scripted requests from this machine (robots.txt read timed out, 14 November
2026), so nothing was requested from it. Every page is the latest Internet Archive capture of
https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/services/foreign-workers/<path>.html, taken unmodified
with the "id_" form (https://web.archive.org/web/<timestamp>id_/<url>); the capture used is recorded in the
note column of index.tsv. web.archive.org was asked at most every 15 s. The date in each file name is the
page's "Date modified" (dcterms.modified).

Saved: 35 HTML pages (all 35 pages the script targets), 1.4 MB, kind guidance, "date modified" 2017-10-16 to
2026-10-27. Checked with `file`; none is an error or challenge page. In the first run of this pass 7 pages
failed because the archive refused connections; a second run fetched them.

Temporary-work agencies, payroll companies, employers of record; EU Blue Card
- "Program requirements for high-wage positions" and "Program requirements for low-wage positions", section
  "Employers" (marked "New: September 18, 2026"): "Note: Staffing or employment agencies who recruit workers
  for other businesses aren't considered employers under the TFW Program. They can't be approved to hire a
  temporary foreign worker for other businesses since an employer-employee relationship doesn't exist." The
  same section says employers won't be approved where an employer-employee relationship doesn't exist and,
  after approval, can't "structure the relationship to avoid payroll, compensation or program requirements".
- The EU Blue Card does not apply to Canada.

How much real guidance
Little in the strict sense: ESDC publishes no officer manual or directive. The published program-requirement
and policy pages are the de facto statement of the assessment criteria, addressed to employers.
