QC Quebec - YES, a true officers' manual. MIFI's Guide des procedures d'immigration (GPI): all 18 sections listed on the live page (gestion de la demande, immigration temporaire, immigration permanente, procedures), the 27 Notes sur les procedures d'immigration (NPI) listed now and the 22 Directives de gestion (2002-2017); plus 7 chapters of the pre-2018 GPI (composante 1, family reunification) from the Wayback Machine. Pages: https://www.quebec.ca/gouvernement/ministeres-organismes/immigration/publications/guide-procedures-immigration , .../notes-procedures-immigration , .../directives-gestion ; files on cdn-contenu.quebec.ca. Archived editions: Wayback captures of www.micc.gouv.qc.ca, www.midi.gouv.qc.ca, www.mifi.gouv.qc.ca /publications/fr/gpi-npi/ and of the cdn-contenu gpi/ folder. The Internet Archive refused connections after 7 files, so the rest of the pre-2018 GPI (composantes 1-5), earlier versions of the current sections and about 100 older NPI are listed in not-obtained.tsv; the capture lists for mifi.gouv.qc.ca and cdn-contenu could not be read at all. BAnQ deposit copies (collections.banq.qc.ca) not used: security page served. French only (the GPI is published only in French).

NU Nunavut - NOTHING TO FIND. Nunavut has no provincial/territorial nominee programme and makes no immigration selection decisions; no page fetched.

How much real guidance is published: two jurisdictions publish real decision-maker manuals: Quebec, which selects its own immigrants under the Canada-Quebec Accord and publishes the full GPI with its procedure notes and management directives, and Newfoundland and Labrador, whose NLPNP Program Policies and Procedures is an internal procedures manual put online. British Columbia, Nova Scotia and the Northwest Territories publish programme guides that set out the criteria officers apply, and BC keeps every dated edition. Manitoba publishes short policy pages, Alberta a few criteria documents, and Ontario nothing beyond applicant information and its regulation. Saskatchewan, New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island and Yukon probably publish guides, but their sites shut out this script (AI-agent robots rule, robots.txt 403, bot check), so nothing was taken from them.

Totals: 240 files, about 81 MB (qc 74, bc 44, nl 82, ns 20, mb 12, nt 5, ab 3), dated 2002-2026. No file over 95 MB.
