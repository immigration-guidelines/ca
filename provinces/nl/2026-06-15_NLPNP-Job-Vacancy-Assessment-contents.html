Job Vacancy Assessment

The Job Vacancy Assessment (JVA) is a provincial labour market test designed to support Newfoundland and Labrador employers in identifying their labour force challenges and evaluating the need to recruit international workers to fill indeterminate, full-time positions in their organizations.

The links below refer to policies and procedures related to the Job Vacancy Assessment. It should be noted that policies and procedures are subject to change without notice.

About the Department

The Department of Jobs, Growth and Rural Development has a mandate to ensure the province has skilled workers for a fast-growing economy. This includes creating a climate which is conducive to economic growth, competitiveness and prosperity. This will be achieved through a spectrum of programs, services, and supports.
