Application for authorization to become a permanent resident under A25, R307 and R66

This section contains policy, procedures and guidance used by IRCC staff. It is posted on the department’s website as a courtesy to stakeholders.

The fee payment will initiate processing.

Please note that for a principal applicant who is a dependent child, this would be an application for humanitarian and compassionate consideration (H&C application), the fees would be $635.

Subsection 25(1) of the Immigration and Refugee Protection Act (IRPA) provides:

“The Minister shall, upon request of a foreign national who is inadmissible or who does not meet the requirements of [the IRPA], and may, on the Minister’s own initiative, examine the circumstances concerning the foreign national and may grant the foreign national permanent resident status or an exemption from any applicable criteria or obligation of [the IRPA] if the Minister is of the opinion that it is justified by humanitarian and compassionate considerations relating to them, taking into account

  • the best interests of a child directly affected, or by
  • public policy considerations.”

In Canada

Foreign nationals may submit a stand-alone H&C consideration application (IMM 0008 and IMM 5283) or a request under subsection A25(1) of the IRPA within a permanent residence application in Canada. The application must be submitted in accordance with the requirements specified in section R10 of the Immigration and Refugee Protection Regulations.

A completed application for authorization to become a permanent resident under subsection A25(1) together with the fees will initiate processing.

Overseas

There is no separate application form overseas. A completed application for acquisition of a permanent residence visa in any of the three main categories, accompanied by a written request for humanitarian and compassionate consideration will initiate processing. The fees paid are consistent with the fee schedule for the types of permanent resident application.

Fee exemptions

Inland there are no fee exemptions. However, overseas the fee exemptions are the same as those for the class under which the client has applied for humanitarian and compassionate consideration. Where the client has been refused under a previous file, but now wishes H&C consideration, the client must reapply for permanent residence and pay the associated fees of the type of application.
