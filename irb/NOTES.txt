What is here
------------
161 indexed files, about 24 MB: 7 Chairperson's Guidelines files (Guidelines 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 9 - the
current set; 1, 5 and 7 have been rescinded or replaced), 9 instructions, 16 jurisprudential-guide
items (designated decisions, policy notes, revocation notices, the policy on guidelines and JGs),
18 policies, 36 procedures/practice notices, 75 legal-services papers and chapters. Six of the rows
are the listing pages themselves (marked "(listing page)" in the title). All kind = guidance.
`date` is the page's dcterms.modified (last change on the site), not the instrument's first issue;
`first_issued` holds dcterms.issued where the page gives it. PDFs have no date in the name.
Pages are bilingual-site English versions; the French versions were not collected.

Not obtained: see not-obtained.tsv (two links on the listings that return HTTP 404, one practice
notice behind the "My Case" portal, HTTP 401). Earlier, superseded versions of the Chairperson's
Guidelines (e.g. Guideline 4 of 1996/2003, Guideline 9 of 2017, rescinded Guidelines 5 and 7) are
not on the site any more and were not sought from web archives.

Assessment
----------
The IRB publishes a great deal of real guidance for its decision-makers: the Chairperson's
Guidelines and jurisprudential guides are formal instruments members must take into account, the
instructions and policies govern how members handle cases, and the Legal Services papers
(refugee definition, credibility, weighing evidence, appeal papers, RAD handbook) are full-length
legal manuals written for members. This folder holds essentially all that is currently published
in English.

History: the folder and script were made by a first agent (all 161 files); a second agent resumed,
verified every file (pdfinfo / HTML check, index = files, no duplicates) and wrote this NOTES.txt.
