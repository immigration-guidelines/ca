Returning appeal-allowed family class files to IRCC for redetermination

This section contains policy, procedures and guidance used by IRCC staff. It is posted on the department’s website as a courtesy to stakeholders.

These instructions provide guidance to IRCC and Canada Border Services Agency (CBSA) staff on returning appeal-allowed family class (FC) files to IRCC for processing after an appeal before the Immigration Appeal Division (IAD).

On this page

  • Summary
  • Background
  • In all cases
  • Fully digitized appeal files
  • Appeal files requiring digitization

Summary

As of May 13, 2024, appeal-allowed FC files are no longer centralized at the Case Processing Centre in Mississauga; they are returned directly to the IRCC processing office that made the original decision. Files containing documents that require digitization are first sent to the New Waterford Digitization Centre (NWDC).

Background

A Canadian citizen or permanent resident who filed an application to sponsor a foreign national as a member of the FC may appeal to the IAD if the application is refused.

If the appeal is allowed, the CBSA hearings officer must return the file to IRCC for processing. The IRCC officer must respect the decision of the IAD, as per subsection 70(1) of the Immigration and Refugee Protection Act (IRPA), and must review the application to determine whether it meets all other eligibility and admissibility requirements.

These instructions outline the steps to be followed by CBSA and IRCC after the IAD has allowed the appeal.

In all cases

The following instructions apply after the IAD has allowed an appeal of an FC sponsorship refusal, whether the IAD’s decision was made after a hearing, by agreement between the parties after alternative dispute resolution or upon the minister’s consent.

Once the IAD has allowed the appeal, the CBSA hearings officer must

  • enter the IAD decision in the Global Case Management System (GCMS) by updating the relevant fields in the IAD tab and the Litigation tab, both found under the Appeals and Litigation tab
  • upload the IAD Reasons and Decision document to GCMS under Incoming Correspondence in the Correspondence tab
    • A paper copy does not need to be sent to IRCC.
  • verify whether the appeal file is fully digitized
    • The appeal file consists of the documents that were originally submitted to IRCC with the sponsorship application and does not include any additional evidence submitted at the IAD hearing.
    • The appeal file is fully digitized if at the start of the appeal process, IRCC notified CBSA that all documents are in GCMS.
    • The appeal file requires digitization if at the start of the appeal process, IRCC provided any paper documents to CBSA for the appeal record.

Fully digitized appeal files

If the appeal file is fully digitized, the CBSA hearings officer must notify the IRCC processing office that made the original decision that the appeal has been allowed.

The hearings officer must

  • determine which IRCC processing office was the decision maker
    • This is the office that appears as the Primary Office in GCMS.
  • consult the IRCC contact information section to identify the email address to be used to contact the decision-making office
  • send an email to the decision-making office (at the email address identified in the previous step) that includes
    • the subject “FC appeal allowed – F#” (with the application number)
    • the application number
    • the full names of the sponsor and of the principal applicant
    • the date of the appeal decision
    • confirmation that all documents in the appeal file are in GCMS
  • add a note in the Notes tab and select the label “Appeals/Litigation” stating that
    • the appeal has been allowed
    • all relevant documents are in GCMS
    • an email has been sent to request that the decision-making IRCC office reopen the application for further processing

The decision-making office then begins redetermination.

Appeal files requiring digitization

If the appeal file requires digitization, the CBSA hearings officer must send the paper documents to the NWDC as well as notify both the NWDC and the IRCC processing office that the appeal has been allowed and that documents have been sent for digitization.

The hearings officer must

  • send all paper documents that were originally submitted by the clients to IRCC (the original visa file) to the NWDC by courier (preferred) or regular mail
  • determine which IRCC processing office was the decision maker
    • This is the office that appears as the Primary Office in GCMS.
  • consult the IRCC Contact Information section to identify the email address to be used to contact the decision-making office
  • send an email to the NWDC copying the decision-making office (at the email address identified in the previous step), and include in the email
    • the subject “FC appeal allowed – documents to be digitized - F#” (with the application number)
    • the application number
    • the full names of the sponsor and of the principal applicant
    • the date of the appeal decision
    • confirmation that the documents requiring digitization have been sent by mail or courier, the tracking # (if applicable) and the date they were sent
  • add a note in the Notes tab and select “Appeals/Litigation”
    • The note should state that
      • the appeal has been allowed
      • the documents requiring digitization have been sent (by mail or courier) to the NWDC
      • an email has been sent to the NWDC to request digitization and the decision-making office was copied on the email

Once the NWDC has received the paper documents sent by mail or courier, the NWDC must

  • add a note in the Notes tab and select “Appeals/Litigation”
    • The note should state that the NWDC has received the documents requiring digitization.
  • digitize the documents
  • reply to the email received from the CBSA, once the documents have been digitized, to advise the IRCC decision-making office that the files have been digitized and the file is ready for redetermination
    • There is no need to include CBSA on the reply.
  • add a note in the Notes tab and select “Appeals/Litigation”
    • The note should state that
      • the documents received from the CBSA have been digitized 
      • an email has been sent to the decision-making office

The decision-making office then begins redetermination.
